# NaPTAN N4A — unresolved-reference contract

Unresolved source references are first-class evidence states, not missing data to be repaired by fabrication.

For a missing StopPoint or StopArea reference, the edge retains the raw publisher identity and deterministic edge key. The corresponding resolved UUID is null, and `resolution_status` is one of `UNRESOLVED_MISSING_NODE`, `UNRESOLVED_MISSING_PLACE`, `UNRESOLVED_MISSING_CHILD`, or `UNRESOLVED_MISSING_PARENT`. Invalid relationship elements use `INVALID` and remain auditable.

The valid portion of a snapshot can be parsed and profiled, but a snapshot is not eligible for `VALIDATED` until graph-level policy accepts its unresolved set. Cycles are a separate `HIERARCHY_CYCLE` validator result and are never hidden by nullable FKs. The N3 counts of 1,543 missing member parents, 41 missing area parents, and 1,581 unresolved references remain measurable; no placeholder source objects are created.
