# National retail and commercial venue toilet feasibility — R5A

## Result

The metadata/sample pass confirms that several major operators expose rich per-site facility information, but it did not establish an openly reusable download, documented public API reuse right, or partnership grant. No commercial operator contributes to the council `READY_NET_NEW` total and no nationwide commercial comparison was performed.

## Useful sample fields

- Tesco: accessible toilets, baby changing, Changing Places and step-free standard toilets are exposed in the official locator vocabulary.
- Asda: official locator filters include accessible toilet, baby changing and Changing Places.
- Sainsbury's: official store pages expose toilets, accessible toilets and baby change.
- IKEA: official UK accessibility guidance identifies accessible toilets across stores and a smaller set of registered Changing Places stores; some access is request/RADAR-key dependent.
- M&S, John Lewis and Lidl samples expose toilets or customer-toilet/accessibility fields, but the bounded pass found no reuse contract.

These observations are evidence that a partnership export could be valuable. They are not permission to crawl locators and do not establish `public_without_purchase`; the conservative default is customer/visitor access or unknown.

## Ranking

1. **Tesco** — broad footprint and richest field vocabulary; request a governed UK branch facility export.
2. **Asda** — broad footprint and similar accessibility/Changing Places fields.
3. **Sainsbury's** — clear store-level facility fields and a tractable partnership request.

IKEA is a strong specialist alternative for high-quality accessibility/Changing Places data despite its smaller store count.

## Partnership request

Ask only for stable branch ID, name, address/postcode, coordinates, ordinary/accessibility/Changing Places/baby-changing flags, store and facility opening hours, access restrictions, last-updated timestamp, licence and attribution. Require explicit confirmation that Relief may store, transform, compare and display the supplied facility evidence.

Classification: **`PERMISSION_REQUIRED`**. Commercial `READY_NET_NEW = 0` in R5A.
